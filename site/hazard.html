Crowd-implied hazard curves

A Polymarket ladder — "will X happen by October / November / December …" — is a cumulative distribution over the event time. Take the survival function, assume a constant intensity between rungs, and you get the same object a CDS curve gives you: an annualised hazard rate per tenor bucket.
