P = mid of best bid/ask on the YES token. For ladders, P is the probability of the event within 12 months, interpolated on the monotone-fitted curve, and λ its average annualised hazard. Credit column = direction for the exposed credit if YES resolves. β = how many price points the headline exposed bond has moved per point of crowd probability (7-day changes, OLS). Grade: A liquid, B tradeable, C thin — rules.

Research tool, not investment advice. Prediction-market prices are what they are: thin, US-centric, and only a probability where someone will trade them — hence the liquidity grade next to every number. Polymarket data via its public API, as-is.
